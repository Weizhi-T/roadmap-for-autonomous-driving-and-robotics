模块化感知预测规划与控制 / Modular Perception, Prediction, Planning, and Control

感知与状态估计 / Perception and State Estimation

系统先估计自身状态、对象、可行驶区域、地图或场景几何，再交给预测与规划模块。ORB-SLAM3是视觉与视觉惯性 SLAM 的代表；BEVFormer体现从多相机时空特征形成 BEV 表示的路线；Occ3D提供三维占据感知的入口。

BEV、占据和对象集合是表示形式。它们可以出现在模块化系统中，也可以参与端到端联合训练。表示是否显式，与训练是否端到端是两个维度。

The system first estimates its own state, objects, drivable areas, maps, or scene geometry, then passes these estimates to prediction and planning modules. ORB-SLAM3 is a representative visual and visual-inertial SLAM system; BEVFormer exemplifies the construction of BEV representations from multi-camera spatiotemporal features; Occ3D provides an entry point into 3D occupancy perception.

BEV, occupancy, and object sets are representation formats. They can appear in modular systems or participate in joint end-to-end training. Whether a representation is explicit and whether training is end-to-end are two separate dimensions.

参与者预测与交互建模 / Agent Prediction and Interaction Modeling

预测周围对象可能的运动与意图，为决策提供条件。MTR是多模态运动预测的代表。需要区分给定历史预测未来，和预测他人对自车新动作的反应；前者表现好不保证后者成立。

Predict the possible motion and intent of surrounding objects to inform decision-making. MTR is a representative approach to multimodal motion prediction. Distinguish predicting the future from a given history from predicting how others will respond to a new ego-vehicle action; strong performance on the former does not guarantee validity for the latter.

搜索优化与反馈控制 / Search, Optimization, and Feedback Control

路径搜索、采样规划、轨迹优化和模型预测控制分别适合不同的状态空间、动力学与约束。MPC 在有限时域求解后只执行一部分，再用新观测重规划。规划输出需经控制器执行，跟踪误差、延迟与执行饱和属于系统性能的一部分。

Path search, sampling-based planning, trajectory optimization, and model predictive control suit different state spaces, dynamics, and constraints. MPC solves a finite-horizon problem, executes only part of the solution, and then replans using new observations. Planning outputs must be executed through a controller; tracking error, latency, and actuator saturation are part of system performance.

直接策略与端到端驾驶 / Direct Policies and End-to-End Driving

单步动作轨迹与动作块 / Single-Step Actions, Trajectories, and Action Chunks

策略可以直接产生转向制动、轨迹航点、关节动作或动作序列。DAVE-2是直接驾驶控制的经典起点；ACT与Diffusion Policy代表机器人动作分块与多模态动作生成。

动作块越长不一定越好：它可以提高推理效率，也可能增加环境变化后的过期动作。对比时需要固定实际闭环频率与总时延。

A policy can directly produce steering and braking commands, trajectory waypoints, joint actions, or action sequences. DAVE-2 is a classic starting point for direct driving control; ACT and Diffusion Policy represent action chunking and multimodal action generation in robotics.

Longer action chunks are not necessarily better: they can improve inference efficiency but may also increase the number of stale actions after the environment changes. Comparisons need to hold the actual closed-loop frequency and total latency fixed.

联合训练的端到端系统 / Jointly Trained End-to-End Systems

UniAD以规划为导向连接感知、预测与规划任务。端到端强调训练或优化的联合程度，不要求所有中间变量都消失。评价应明确哪些模块训练时联合、哪些部署时保留，以及规划收益依赖哪些中间监督。

UniAD connects perception, prediction, and planning tasks with a planning-oriented objective. End-to-end refers to the degree of joint training or optimization; it does not require all intermediate variables to disappear. Evaluation should specify which modules are trained jointly, which remain at deployment, and which intermediate supervision the planning gains depend on.

视觉语言动作模型 / Vision-Language-Action Models

视觉语言骨干与动作接口 / Vision-Language Backbones and Action Interfaces

VLA 将视觉与语言条件接入动作生成。RT-2把动作 token 与视觉语言知识迁移结合；OpenVLA提供开放模型与适配研究入口；π0结合视觉语言骨干与连续动作专家。

VLA 是一类接口和模型组织方式，内部可以使用不同训练算法、动作头、记忆和安全模块。不能仅凭输入里有语言就推断有长程规划或可靠推理。

VLA models condition action generation on vision and language. RT-2 combines action tokens with the transfer of vision-language knowledge; OpenVLA provides an open model and an entry point into adaptation research; π0 combines a vision-language backbone with a continuous-action expert.

VLA denotes a family of interfaces and model organizations whose internals can use different training algorithms, action heads, memory, and safety modules. The presence of language in the input alone does not imply long-horizon planning or reliable reasoning.

动作头的交叉选择 / Cross-Cutting Choices of Action Heads

- 自回归动作 token：生成次序与动作编码是核心选择，代表 RT-2 与 OpenVLA。

- 连续动作分布：扩散策略或流匹配动作专家，代表 Diffusion Policy 与 π0。

- 直接回归或动作分块：可作为简单强基线，也可作为大型骨干后的执行头。

Flow Matching是生成训练方法，VLA 是应用系统族。比较动作头时，要匹配数据、骨干、采样预算和执行接口。

- Autoregressive action tokens: generation order and action encoding are central design choices, as exemplified by RT-2 and OpenVLA.

- Continuous action distributions: diffusion policies or flow-matching action experts, as exemplified by Diffusion Policy and π0.

- Direct regression or action chunking: these can serve as simple, strong baselines or as execution heads attached to large backbones.

Flow Matching is a generative training method, whereas VLA is a family of application systems. When comparing action heads, match the data, backbone, sampling budget, and execution interface.

快慢分层与异步执行 / Fast–Slow Hierarchies and Asynchronous Execution

GR00T N1展示不同时间尺度的系统组织；Real-Time Chunking研究动作分块在异步执行时的衔接。快慢分层回答谁多久更新一次，异步 chunk 回答新旧动作计划如何交接，二者可组合但不是同一技术。

必须说明观测时间、推理开始与结束时间、队列长度、不可撤回的动作前缀、取消或切换条件。只报告网络 forward 时间不足以证明实时性。

GR00T N1 demonstrates system organization across different timescales; Real-Time Chunking studies how to connect action chunks during asynchronous execution. A fast–slow hierarchy determines which component updates at what frequency, whereas asynchronous chunking determines how old and new action plans hand over execution. The two can be combined, but they are not the same technique.

Specify observation times, inference start and end times, queue lengths, irrevocable action prefixes, and cancellation or switching conditions. Reporting only the network’s forward-pass time is insufficient to establish real-time operation.

世界模型驱动的行动 / World-Model-Driven Action

前向模型与在线搜索 / Forward Models and Online Search

先预测候选动作的后果，再用采样、优化或 MPC 选动作。PETS是模型学习与控制的经典入口；V-JEPA 2包含动作条件建模与控制研究。世界模型可以位于像素、对象或潜空间，表示选择与规划算法独立。

First predict the consequences of candidate actions, then select actions through sampling, optimization, or MPC. PETS is a classic entry point into model learning and control; V-JEPA 2 includes research on action-conditioned modeling and control. A world model can operate in pixel, object, or latent space; the choice of representation is independent of the planning algorithm.

子目标与低层实现 / Subgoals and Low-Level Realization

高层生成中间状态、图像或语义子目标，低层通过目标条件策略、逆动力学或前向模型搜索实现。三种低层接口具有不同的可达域、延迟和误差来源，不应合并计算成绩。

高层预测的状态是否合理、当前控制器是否做得到、整个任务是否更容易完成，要分别评测。一个好看的未来图像不是动作可执行性的证书。

A high-level module generates intermediate states, images, or semantic subgoals, which a low-level module realizes through a goal-conditioned policy, inverse dynamics, or search with a forward model. These three low-level interfaces have different reachable sets, latencies, and sources of error, so their scores should not be pooled.

Evaluate separately whether the high-level predicted state is plausible, whether the current controller can achieve it, and whether it makes the overall task easier to complete. An appealing image of the future is not a certificate of action executability.

世界动作联合模型 / Joint World–Action Models

WorldVLA联合未来图像预测和动作生成，是理解世界与动作联合建模的代表。WAM 或 WA 常用于描述这类联系，但目前不是一个唯一、统一的标准架构名。归类应写清预测什么、是否动作条件、如何产生动作、是否在线搜索。

WorldVLA jointly predicts future images and generates actions, providing a representative example for understanding joint world–action modeling. WAM or WA is often used to describe such connections, but neither currently denotes a single, unified standard architecture. Classification should specify what is predicted, whether the prediction is action-conditioned, how actions are produced, and whether online search is used.

分层策略与技能编排 / Hierarchical Policies and Skill Orchestration

技能定义与接口 / Skill Definitions and Interfaces

技能可以来自控制器、模仿学习、强化学习或程序。Options 框架把时间上扩展的动作组织为启动条件、内部策略与终止条件。技能库要给出输入、前置条件、成功判据、超时和恢复方式。

agentic 描述选择、调用、反馈与重规划的组织方式，不必使用语言模型。行为树、有限状态机与分层策略都可作为清晰可检验的基线。

Skills can come from controllers, imitation learning, reinforcement learning, or programs. The Options framework organizes temporally extended actions into initiation conditions, an internal policy, and termination conditions. A skill library should specify inputs, preconditions, success criteria, timeouts, and recovery procedures.

“Agentic” describes how selection, invocation, feedback, and replanning are organized; it does not require a language model. Behavior trees, finite-state machines, and hierarchical policies can all serve as clear, testable baselines.

技能选择与组合 / Skill Selection and Composition

SayCan把语言任务相关性与技能可执行性结合；Code as Policies让语言模型生成调用感知与控制 API 的程序；VoxPoser结合语言与空间价值图组织控制。它们使用的执行接口不同，成绩不宜按“有没有 LLM”简单归因。

SayCan combines language-based task relevance with skill executability; Code as Policies uses language models to generate programs that call perception and control APIs; VoxPoser combines language and spatial value maps to organize control. Their execution interfaces differ, so performance should not be attributed simply to “whether an LLM is used.”

反馈重规划与记忆 / Feedback-Driven Replanning and Memory

Inner Monologue将环境反馈接入任务执行与规划。经验检索、记忆、程序修订和技能更新可以发生在执行间隙，但要分别说明写入的是外部知识还是模型参数，以及更新是否在后续任务之间共享。

Inner Monologue incorporates environmental feedback into task execution and planning. Experience retrieval, memory, program revision, and skill updates can take place between execution steps, but specify separately whether updates are written to external knowledge or model parameters, and whether they are shared across subsequent tasks.

求助与能力边界 / Help-Seeking and Capability Limits

KnowNo是校准不确定性与求助决策的入口。系统说“不确定”、降低速度、调用恢复技能和请求人类帮助是不同动作。校准保障依赖数据与分布条件，不宜解释为系统具有普遍自我认知。

KnowNo is an entry point into uncertainty calibration and help-seeking decisions. Saying “uncertain,” slowing down, invoking a recovery skill, and requesting human help are different actions. Calibration guarantees depend on data and distributional conditions and should not be interpreted as general self-awareness on the part of the system.

安全约束与部署监督 / Safety Constraints and Deployment Supervision

显式约束与稳定性 / Explicit Constraints and Stability

CBF可以将安全集合约束接入控制；RSS提供形式化驾驶安全建模的代表。稳定性、收敛性、约束满足与任务成功是不同性质，必须给出各自假设和结论。

任何保障都要绑定动力学模型、状态估计、约束可行性、采样与延迟、外部参与者假设。安全过滤器的存在不能自动证明整个感知学习系统安全。

CBF can incorporate safe-set constraints into control; RSS is a representative approach to formal driving-safety modeling. Stability, convergence, constraint satisfaction, and task success are distinct properties, each requiring its own assumptions and conclusions.

Any guarantee must be tied to assumptions about the dynamics model, state estimation, constraint feasibility, sampling and latency, and external agents. The presence of a safety filter does not automatically establish the safety of the entire perception-and-learning system.

调度监测与恢复 / Scheduling, Monitoring, and Recovery

部署监督包括动作合法性检查、截止时间、资源监测、故障检测、回退控制器和人工接管接口。学习模型之外的系统组织会改变真实表现，应纳入同预算对照，而不是当作无成本的附加模块。

Deployment supervision includes action-validity checks, deadlines, resource monitoring, fault detection, fallback controllers, and human-takeover interfaces. System organization outside the learned model changes real-world performance and should be included in comparisons under matched budgets rather than treated as a cost-free add-on.
