开环与离线评测 / Open-Loop and Offline Evaluation

感知预测与动作指标 / Perception, Prediction, and Action Metrics

检测、分割、定位、运动预测、动作重建与轨迹误差有助于定位模块问题。它们通常基于固定日志或标注，无法完整观察模型自己的动作怎样改变后续状态。

同一个轨迹误差可能来自可接受的多解，也可能隐藏危险动作；同样的感知误差在不同决策场景中的代价也不同。因此开环指标应与决策影响建立联系。

Detection, segmentation, localization, motion prediction, action reconstruction, and trajectory error help locate problems within modules. They are usually based on fixed logs or annotations and cannot fully reveal how the model’s own actions alter subsequent states.

A given trajectory error may reflect acceptable alternative solutions, or it may conceal dangerous actions; the cost of the same perception error also varies across decision contexts. Open-loop metrics should therefore be connected to their effects on decisions.

非交互式规划评测 / Non-Interactive Planning Evaluation

NAVSIM代表以数据驱动的非交互式评测研究规划能力。使用环境模型或短时展开，并不自动使评测成为带完整相互响应的交互闭环。阅读时检查自车、他车、传感器和行为是否随干预更新。

NAVSIM represents research that studies planning capabilities through data-driven, non-interactive evaluation. Using an environment model or a short-horizon rollout does not automatically make an evaluation an interactive closed loop with fully reciprocal responses. Check whether the ego vehicle, other vehicles, sensors, and behaviors are updated in response to interventions.

固定数据的离线强化学习评测 / Offline Reinforcement Learning Evaluation with Fixed Data

D4RL提供任务、数据集与评测协议入口。数据覆盖、采集策略和归一化定义会影响分数；不同 benchmark 的数字不能直接合并成通用能力排名。

D4RL provides an entry point into tasks, datasets, and evaluation protocols. Data coverage, collection policies, and normalization definitions affect scores; numbers from different benchmarks cannot be directly pooled into a ranking of general capability.

闭环仿真与真实任务 / Closed-Loop Simulation and Real-World Tasks

驾驶闭环 / Closed-Loop Driving

nuPlan研究基于真实数据的规划评测；CARLA Leaderboard与Bench2Drive提供驾驶闭环测试入口。对比分数前先核对版本、路线、天气、交通、传感器、地图信息和参与者反应模型。

闭环结果也有层次：日志重放、可响应代理、完整传感器再生成及真实道路测试的证据范围不同。应写清干预影响了哪些后续变量。

nuPlan studies planning evaluation based on real-world data; CARLA Leaderboard and Bench2Drive provide entry points into closed-loop driving tests. Before comparing scores, check versions, routes, weather, traffic, sensors, map information, and agent-response models.

Closed-loop results also have distinct levels: log replay, responsive agents, full sensor regeneration, and real-road testing provide evidence with different scopes. Specify which subsequent variables are affected by interventions.

机器人任务与长程执行 / Robot Tasks and Long-Horizon Execution

LIBERO提供机器人学习任务与迁移研究入口；CALVIN关注语言条件长程任务；SIMPLER研究以仿真评估真实机器人策略。成功率须绑定任务定义、场景、尝试次数、复位和模型输入。

单次成功视频只说明发生过成功，不估计总体成功率。长程任务应记录中途恢复、重试、失败位置和完成时间。

LIBERO provides an entry point into robot learning tasks and transfer research; CALVIN focuses on language-conditioned long-horizon tasks; SIMPLER studies the use of simulation to evaluate real-world robot policies. Success rates must be tied to task definitions, scenes, numbers of attempts, resets, and model inputs.

A video of a single successful trial only shows that success occurred; it does not estimate the overall success rate. Long-horizon tasks should record recovery during execution, retries, failure locations, and completion times.

真实部署与外部分布 / Real-World Deployment and Distribution Shifts

将场景、对象、语言、机体和扰动逐项改变，观察泛化边界。仿真验证后的真实测试仍需独立设计，尤其检查视觉差异、摩擦接触、标定、控制频率和人类干预。

测试应记录样本量、随机种子或场景集合、版本与不确定性。小样本中没有事故不能作为稀有风险已经足够低的证据。

Vary scenes, objects, language, embodiments, and disturbances one at a time to observe the limits of generalization. Real-world tests after simulation validation still require an independent design, with particular attention to visual differences, friction and contact, calibration, control frequency, and human intervention.

Tests should record sample sizes, random seeds or scenario sets, versions, and uncertainty. The absence of accidents in a small sample is not evidence that rare-event risk is sufficiently low.

安全可靠性与形式化分析 / Safety, Reliability, and Formal Analysis

约束满足与风险场景 / Constraint Satisfaction and Risk Scenarios

碰撞、越界、力矩、接触力、安全距离和任务约束应按应用定义。CBF与RSS展示显式安全结构，但理论结论依赖具体假设。实测低违例率、模型内约束成立和真实系统安全是不同层级的证据。

Collisions, boundary violations, torques, contact forces, safety distances, and task constraints should be defined for the application. CBF and RSS demonstrate explicit safety structures, but their theoretical conclusions depend on specific assumptions. Low measured violation rates, constraint satisfaction within a model, and real-system safety are different levels of evidence.

鲁棒性与运行时边界 / Robustness and Runtime Limits

测试传感器失效、延迟抖动、观测过期、计算超时、动作饱和与外部扰动。要同时检查性能下降、监测是否及时发现，以及回退或恢复是否仍然可靠。

Test sensor failures, latency jitter, stale observations, computation timeouts, actuator saturation, and external disturbances. Examine performance degradation, whether monitoring detects problems promptly, and whether fallback or recovery remains reliable.

校准与求助 / Calibration and Help-Seeking

置信度应该与正确性、可行性或风险的具体事件对应。KnowNo提供求助与不确定性校准的代表；分布变化后应重新检验校准，不能将一个分数当作跨任务通用的安全证书。

Confidence should correspond to a specific event involving correctness, feasibility, or risk. KnowNo is a representative approach to help-seeking and uncertainty calibration. Reassess calibration after distribution shifts; a single score cannot serve as a general safety certificate across tasks.

失败归因与下一轮改进 / Failure Attribution and Next-Iteration Improvements

从结果到机制 / From Outcomes to Mechanisms

对失败至少记录观测、模型版本、关键中间量、动作、环境响应和恢复结果。可分为数据缺口、不可观测状态、表征错误、目标或奖励偏差、规划错误、执行失配和部署故障。

分类是待验证的诊断，不是给每次失败贴一个确定标签。用受控干预检验：补观测是否改善，换正确状态是否改善，固定策略只换控制器是否改善。

For each failure, record at least the observations, model version, key intermediate variables, actions, environmental responses, and recovery outcomes. Possible categories include data gaps, unobservable states, representation errors, objective or reward misspecification, planning errors, execution mismatches, and deployment faults.

These categories are diagnoses to be tested, not definitive labels to attach to every failure. Use controlled interventions to test whether adding observations helps, substituting the correct state helps, or replacing only the controller while holding the policy fixed helps.

把失败送回正确环节 / Route Failures Back to the Appropriate Stage

- 数据不足：补采、重标注、筛选、仿真或生成。

- 学习目标不合适：改变监督、奖励、损失或训练配方。

- 执行接口不匹配：修正坐标、时延、技能前置条件、计划切换与恢复。

- 评测失真：调整场景、交互模型、指标和数据切分。

先通过小而可控的对照确定方向，再扩展系统规模。收益若只是来自更长训练或更多数据，应如实归因。

- Insufficient data: collect more data, relabel, filter, simulate, or generate it.

- Unsuitable learning objectives: change supervision, rewards, losses, or the training recipe.

- Mismatched execution interfaces: correct coordinates, latency, skill preconditions, plan switching, and recovery.

- Distorted evaluation: revise scenarios, interaction models, metrics, and data splits.

First establish a direction through small, controlled comparisons, then scale the system. If gains come only from longer training or more data, attribute them accordingly.

更新接受与持续学习 / Update Acceptance and Continual Learning

回归测试与版本记录 / Regression Testing and Version Records

每次更新绑定数据、代码、权重、环境、提示或技能库版本。新任务收益与旧任务退化同时报告，保护不参与开发的测试集。根据失败影响范围安排重点回归，但不要把局部测试当作全域保障。

Tie every update to versions of the data, code, weights, environment, prompts, or skill library. Report gains on new tasks together with degradation on old tasks, and protect test sets that are not used in development. Prioritize regression tests according to the scope of a failure’s impact, but do not treat local testing as a guarantee across the entire operating domain.

接受拒绝与回滚 / Acceptance, Rejection, and Rollback

预先定义更新门槛：任务收益、风险变化、延迟与资源成本、旧能力保持。存在不确定性时可以保留候选并扩大验证；不满足门槛就拒绝或回滚，而不是只保留最好看的指标。

Define update acceptance criteria in advance: task gains, changes in risk, latency and resource costs, and retention of prior capabilities. When uncertainty remains, retain a candidate and expand validation; if it fails to meet the criteria, reject it or roll back rather than retain only the most favorable metrics.

经验与程序更新 / Experience and Program Updates

外部记忆、技能图和程序也需要版本与评测。它们可以改善行为，但更新并不等于模型权重获得新能力。评测应区分冻结记忆、允许在线写入、跨任务共享和不同任务顺序。

External memory, skill graphs, and programs also need versioning and evaluation. They can improve behavior, but updating them does not mean that model weights have acquired new capabilities. Evaluation should distinguish frozen memory, permission to write online, sharing across tasks, and different task orders.
