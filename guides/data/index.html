真实采集与示教 / Real-World Collection and Demonstrations

驾驶传感器日志 / Driving Sensor Logs

车辆记录相机、LiDAR、雷达、定位与控制等信号，经时间同步、标定和场景切分形成数据。nuScenes与Waymo Open Dataset是理解多传感器驾驶数据结构的经典入口。读取时先检查任务分支、传感器配置、标注和数据划分，不把一个数据集的检测能力外推为规划能力。

关键问题是覆盖：地区、天气、道路参与者、罕见事件和采集策略都会塑造分布。正常驾驶日志包含大量相似片段，按帧随机划分可能让相邻序列泄漏到测试集。

Vehicles record signals from cameras, LiDAR, radar, localization, and control systems; time synchronization, calibration, and scene segmentation turn these recordings into datasets. nuScenes and the Waymo Open Dataset are classic starting points for understanding the structure of multisensor driving data. When examining a dataset, first check its task tracks, sensor configuration, annotations, and splits. Do not extrapolate evidence of detection capability on a dataset into evidence of planning capability.

Coverage is the central issue: geography, weather, road users, rare events, and collection strategies all shape the distribution. Normal-driving logs contain many similar segments, and random frame-level splits can allow neighboring sequences to leak into the test set.

机器人遥操作与动作示教 / Robot Teleoperation and Action Demonstrations

人通过手柄、主从装置或可穿戴接口给出动作，机器人同步记录观测和状态。ALOHA兼具低成本双臂遥操作与 ACT 策略学习；DROID提供多样场景下的真实机器人示教；Open X-Embodiment 与 RT-X讨论跨机器人数据与策略。

跨机体训练需要明确动作单位、坐标系、控制频率、自由度和夹爪语义。把数据放进统一格式，并不自动消除不同机体的可达性和执行差异。

Humans provide actions through controllers, master–slave devices, or wearable interfaces while the robot synchronously records observations and states. ALOHA combines low-cost bimanual teleoperation with ACT policy learning; DROID provides real-robot demonstrations across diverse settings; Open X-Embodiment and RT-X address cross-robot data and policies.

Cross-embodiment training requires explicit definitions of action units, coordinate frames, control frequencies, degrees of freedom, and gripper semantics. Putting data into a unified format does not automatically eliminate differences in reachability and execution across embodiments.

人类视频与便携采集 / Human Video and Portable Data Collection

UMI通过手持采集接口降低与特定机器人硬件绑定的采集成本。更一般的人类视频可提供对象关系、任务过程和视觉先验，但常缺少机器人执行所需的力、关节状态和动作标签。因此要区分“看过任务过程”和“获得当前机体的动作监督”。

UMI uses a handheld collection interface to reduce data-collection costs tied to specific robot hardware. More general human video can provide object relationships, task procedures, and visual priors, but often lacks the forces, joint states, and action labels needed for robot execution. It is therefore necessary to distinguish “observing how a task unfolds” from “obtaining action supervision for the current embodiment.”

交互纠错与主动补采 / Interactive Correction and Active Data Collection

DAgger让学习策略访问自己会遇到的状态，再向专家查询相应动作，缓解单纯模仿中训练状态与部署状态分布不一致的问题。它同时属于数据闭环与交互式模仿学习；归类时标记两个位置即可，不必强行选一个。

主动学习、难例挖掘与人工接管用于决定采什么、标什么。判断增量应比较同标注预算下的随机采样、简单错误筛选和新策略，并记录查询成本。

DAgger lets a learned policy visit states it will encounter and then queries an expert for the corresponding actions, mitigating the mismatch between training-state and deployment-state distributions in pure imitation. It belongs to both the data feedback loop and interactive imitation learning; mark both locations when classifying it rather than forcing it into only one.

Active learning, hard-example mining, and human takeovers help determine what to collect and annotate. To assess incremental value, compare random sampling, simple error-based filtering, and the new strategy under the same annotation budget, and record query costs.

质量控制与数据配方 / Quality Control and Data Recipes

同步标定标注与切分 / Synchronization, Calibration, Annotation, and Splitting

数据处理至少要检查时间戳、传感器内外参、动作坐标与控制时延、异常帧、标注一致性，以及训练和测试之间是否共享相近场景。语言标注、轨迹标签、奖励标签和事件标签回答不同问题。

每个数据版本应保留采集条件、处理步骤、许可证和切分方式。衡量数据质量时既看标注正确性，也看对最终任务的覆盖与影响。

Data processing should at minimum check timestamps, sensor intrinsics and extrinsics, action coordinates and control latency, anomalous frames, annotation consistency, and whether the training and test sets share closely related scenes. Language annotations, trajectory labels, reward labels, and event labels answer different questions.

Each data version should retain its collection conditions, processing steps, license, and split procedure. Assessing data quality requires examining both annotation correctness and the coverage and impact relevant to the final task.

数据筛选混合与课程 / Data Selection, Mixing, and Curricula

可按质量、任务、机体、失败类型与难度对数据重采样。目标是提高有效覆盖，而不只是增加数据量。跨域混合需要分别报告不同域的收益与退化，防止总体平均数掩盖稀缺任务受损。

新增数据的价值应通过固定模型和训练预算的对照估计。若同时改变模型、数据规模与训练时长，就难以判断是哪个环节带来改进。

Data can be resampled by quality, task, embodiment, failure type, and difficulty. The aim is to improve effective coverage, not merely increase data volume. Cross-domain mixtures require separate reporting of gains and degradation in each domain so that overall averages do not conceal harm to tasks with scarce data.

Estimate the value of additional data through controlled comparisons with a fixed model and training budget. If the model, dataset scale, and training duration change simultaneously, it becomes difficult to determine which component produced the improvement.

物理仿真与程序化生成 / Physics Simulation and Procedural Generation

物理与行为仿真 / Physics and Behavior Simulation

CARLA面向驾驶环境；Isaac Sim提供机器人仿真；Isaac Lab是建立在仿真生态之上的机器人学习框架。框架、渲染器、动力学引擎和训练算法是不同层，使用同一工具不意味着使用相同训练方法。

仿真适合控制初始状态、随机种子与干预，尤其方便形成闭环训练数据。关键边界是仿真与真实世界在传感器、接触、摩擦、延迟和参与者反应上的偏差。

CARLA targets driving environments; Isaac Sim provides robotics simulation; Isaac Lab is a robot-learning framework built on a simulation ecosystem. Frameworks, renderers, dynamics engines, and training algorithms occupy different layers. Using the same tool does not imply using the same training method.

Simulation is well suited to controlling initial states, random seeds, and interventions, and is particularly convenient for producing closed-loop training data. Its central limitation is the discrepancy between simulation and the real world in sensors, contact, friction, latency, and the responses of other participants.

域随机化与程序化场景 / Domain Randomization and Procedural Scenes

Domain Randomization通过随机纹理、光照、物体和其他参数拓展训练分布；Scenic用程序化描述组织场景与约束。前者主要决定变化范围，后者可以表达场景的结构与条件。

随机化范围太窄会漏掉部署变化，太宽则可能消耗样本在不相关或不合理场景上。要检验真实任务性能，不以生成了多少场景作为充分证据。

Domain Randomization broadens the training distribution by randomizing textures, lighting, objects, and other parameters; Scenic organizes scenes and constraints through procedural descriptions. The former primarily determines the range of variation, while the latter can express scene structure and conditions.

A randomization range that is too narrow misses deployment variations; one that is too broad may spend samples on irrelevant or implausible scenes. Evaluate performance on real-world tasks rather than treating the number of generated scenes as sufficient evidence.

示教轨迹与任务扩增 / Demonstration Trajectory and Task Augmentation

MimicGen从少量示教生成更多机器人操作数据。扩增依赖任务分解、物体与动作关系及仿真执行条件，不能把任意变形后的轨迹都视为有效专家示教。

MimicGen generates additional robot manipulation data from a small number of demonstrations. Augmentation depends on task decomposition, object–action relationships, and simulation execution conditions; arbitrary transformations of trajectories cannot all be treated as valid expert demonstrations.

三维重建与动态渲染 / 3D Reconstruction and Dynamic Rendering

NeRF与三维高斯 / NeRF and 3D Gaussians

NeRF通过辐射场表示场景，3D Gaussian Splatting以显式高斯基元实现高效新视角合成。它们回答如何从观测重建与渲染场景，可以作为数据生成或仿真的视觉基础。

NeRF represents scenes through radiance fields, while 3D Gaussian Splatting uses explicit Gaussian primitives for efficient novel-view synthesis. They address how to reconstruct and render scenes from observations and can serve as the visual foundation for data generation or simulation.

动态四维表示 / Dynamic 4D Representations

4D Gaussian Splatting将动态场景变化纳入表示。动态渲染能够还原随时间变化的外观与几何，但录像中的运动重放与“对新动作作出合理反应”仍是不同能力。

4D Gaussian Splatting incorporates dynamic scene changes into the representation. Dynamic rendering can reconstruct appearance and geometry that change over time, but replaying motion from a recording and “responding plausibly to new actions” remain different capabilities.

重建与交互的接口 / Interfaces between Reconstruction and Interaction

可把真实日志重建、可编辑对象、行为模型和物理约束组合成仿真管线。检验时分别检查新视角质量、几何一致性、对象运动、动作干预响应和下游策略收益。一个环节通过不能替代其他环节。

Real-world log reconstruction, editable objects, behavior models, and physical constraints can be combined into a simulation pipeline. Evaluation should separately examine novel-view quality, geometric consistency, object motion, responses to action interventions, and downstream policy gains. Passing one check cannot substitute for passing the others.

学习型生成与世界模型 / Learned Generation and World Models

视频与多模态生成 / Video and Multimodal Generation

GAIA-1与DriveDreamer是驾驶生成模型入口，UniSim探索通过真实世界交互模拟支持智能体学习。生成器可用于数据扩增、场景编辑或交互建模，具体用途取决于模型条件输入和训练目标。

GAIA-1 and DriveDreamer provide starting points for driving generative models; UniSim explores supporting agent learning by simulating real-world interactions. Generators can be used for data augmentation, scene editing, or interaction modeling; their specific use depends on the model’s conditioning inputs and training objective.

像素预测与潜空间预测 / Pixel Prediction and Latent-Space Prediction

世界模型可以预测未来图像、对象状态或潜变量。I-JEPA与V-JEPA代表联合嵌入预测表征学习；V-JEPA 2 及动作条件模型进一步提供学习与控制的研究入口。原始 JEPA 表征目标本身没有证明动作条件规划能力。

World models can predict future images, object states, or latent variables. I-JEPA and V-JEPA represent joint-embedding predictive representation learning; V-JEPA 2 and its action-conditioned model provide a further entry point into learning and control research. The original JEPA representation objective does not by itself establish action-conditioned planning capability.

三种不同的合格标准 / Three Distinct Standards of Adequacy

- 训练数据增强： 新数据是否在固定预算下改善真实或目标域任务表现。

- 规划与候选动作排序： 改变动作后，模型是否能保留足够准确的相对后果，帮助选出更好的行动。

- 安全与风险评估： 场景概率、交互反应和失效分布是否与真实风险有可信联系。

视觉逼真、多相机一致或较低预测误差都不能单独满足第三项。世界模型参与闭环时，还应防止策略利用模型误差取得虚高回报。

- Training-data augmentation: Does the new data improve task performance in the real world or target domain under a fixed budget?

- Planning and candidate-action ranking: When actions change, does the model preserve sufficiently accurate relative consequences to help select better actions?

- Safety and risk assessment: Do scene probabilities, interaction responses, and failure distributions have a credible connection to real-world risk?

Visual realism, multicamera consistency, or low prediction error cannot individually satisfy the third standard. When a world model participates in a closed loop, it is also necessary to prevent the policy from exploiting model errors to obtain artificially high returns.
