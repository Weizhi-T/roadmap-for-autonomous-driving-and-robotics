监督学习与模仿学习 / Supervised Learning and Imitation Learning

行为克隆与SFT / Behavior Cloning and SFT

行为克隆根据示教观测预测专家动作；SFT 表示在监督样本上微调已有模型。在机器人或驾驶场景中，SFT 的目标可以是动作、轨迹、语言解释或多个目标的组合。DAVE-2是驾驶端到端监督学习的经典入口，ACT展示动作分块的模仿学习。

训练误差小仍可能在部署后逐步偏离示教分布。应比较闭环表现、恢复能力与扰动鲁棒性，并检查不同动作参数化造成的误差含义差异。

Behavior cloning predicts expert actions from demonstration observations; SFT refers to fine-tuning an existing model on supervised examples. In robotics or driving, SFT targets can be actions, trajectories, language explanations, or combinations of objectives. DAVE-2 is a classic starting point for end-to-end supervised learning in driving, and ACT demonstrates imitation learning with action chunking.

Even with low training error, a policy may progressively drift away from the demonstration distribution after deployment. Compare closed-loop performance, recovery capability, and robustness to perturbations, and examine how different action parameterizations change the meaning of prediction errors.

交互式模仿 / Interactive Imitation

DAgger收集当前策略访问状态的专家标签，再聚合数据迭代训练。它增加了对部署分布的覆盖，但需要专家查询和可接受的交互成本。应报告查询次数、接管规则和训练迭代。

DAgger collects expert labels for states visited by the current policy, then aggregates the data for iterative training. It increases coverage of the deployment distribution but requires expert queries and acceptable interaction costs. Report the number of queries, takeover rules, and training iterations.

逆强化学习与对抗模仿 / Inverse Reinforcement Learning and Adversarial Imitation

这类方法从示教中推断奖励或直接匹配行为分布。GAIL通过对抗目标进行模仿。与行为克隆相比，训练信号和交互需求不同；不能用“没有逐步动作标签损失”推断其没有专家信息依赖。

These methods infer rewards from demonstrations or directly match behavior distributions. GAIL performs imitation through an adversarial objective. Their training signals and interaction requirements differ from those of behavior cloning; the absence of a stepwise action-label loss does not imply the absence of a dependence on expert information.

自监督表征与多模态对齐 / Self-Supervised Representations and Multimodal Alignment

重建式学习 / Reconstruction-Based Learning

MAE以被遮挡内容的重建为学习信号。像素重建可以学到有用表征，但会同时关注任务相关与无关细节；最终仍要在控制任务上验证表征是否有用。

MAE uses reconstruction of masked content as its learning signal. Pixel reconstruction can learn useful representations, but it attends to both task-relevant and task-irrelevant details; the usefulness of the representation must ultimately be validated on control tasks.

对比学习与表征对齐 / Contrastive Learning and Representation Alignment

CLIP通过图文匹配组织联合表示。机器人中还可以对齐视觉、语言、动作与状态，但需要检查跨模态正负样本、时序对应和迁移任务。表征相近是训练目标，不能直接作为物理可执行性证明。

CLIP organizes joint representations through image–text matching. Robotics can also align vision, language, actions, and states, but this requires examining cross-modal positive and negative examples, temporal correspondence, and transfer tasks. Similarity between representations is a training objective, not direct evidence of physical executability.

联合嵌入预测 / Joint-Embedding Prediction

I-JEPA在表示空间预测目标区域，V-JEPA将预测扩展到视频时空信息。与像素重建相比，这一路线更关注预测抽象表示；是否编码可控因素以及如何接入动作，取决于具体设计。V-JEPA 2应进一步区分无动作预训练部分与动作条件控制部分。

I-JEPA predicts target regions in representation space; V-JEPA extends prediction to spatiotemporal video information. Compared with pixel reconstruction, this approach focuses more on predicting abstract representations; whether it encodes controllable factors and how actions are incorporated depend on the specific design. V-JEPA 2 should further be separated into its action-free pretraining component and its action-conditioned control component.

输出分布与生成目标 / Output Distributions and Generative Objectives

直接回归与动作分块 / Direct Regression and Action Chunking

可直接预测单步动作、航点或一段动作序列。动作分块减少高频反复决策的负担，但开环执行更长的块也会延后对新观测的响应。ACT可用于理解动作分块与时序聚合。

Models can directly predict a single-step action, waypoints, or a sequence of actions. Action chunking reduces the burden of repeated high-frequency decisions, but executing longer chunks open-loop also delays responses to new observations. ACT provides a way to understand action chunking and temporal aggregation.

自回归动作生成 / Autoregressive Action Generation

RT-2把动作表示为 token，与视觉语言学习结合；OpenVLA提供开放 VLA 研究入口。自回归描述生成顺序，动作词表、连续值编码和序列长度会影响精度与延迟。

RT-2 represents actions as tokens and combines them with vision-language learning; OpenVLA provides an open entry point for VLA research. Autoregression describes the generation order; the action vocabulary, continuous-value encoding, and sequence length affect accuracy and latency.

扩散与流匹配 / Diffusion and Flow Matching

Diffusion Policy学习条件动作分布并通过去噪采样生成动作；π0将视觉语言模型与流匹配动作专家结合。Flow Matching 原始方法提供流匹配的基础定义。扩散、流匹配和自回归是输出建模选择，可以与监督学习、不同骨干和后训练结合，不能与 PPO 或 SFT 混成一个算法层级。

比较时匹配观测、数据、执行频率、采样步数与总延迟。多模态动作分布的表达能力只有在实际闭环任务中才转化为收益。

Diffusion Policy learns conditional action distributions and generates actions through denoising-based sampling; π0 combines a vision-language model with a flow-matching action expert. The original Flow Matching method provides the foundational definitions of flow matching. Diffusion, flow matching, and autoregression are choices for output modeling that can be combined with supervised learning, different backbones, and post-training. They should not be conflated with PPO or SFT at a single algorithmic level.

Comparisons should match observations, data, execution frequencies, sampling steps, and total latency. The expressive capacity of multimodal action distributions only becomes a benefit when it improves actual closed-loop tasks.

强化学习 / Reinforcement Learning

在线无模型强化学习 / Online Model-Free Reinforcement Learning

策略与环境交互，通过回报优化行为。PPO是常用的 on-policy 策略优化算法；SAC是最大熵框架下常用的 off-policy 算法。on-policy 与 off-policy 描述更新如何使用采样分布，与“部署时在线学习”不是同一个概念。

PPO 或 SAC 可以在仿真中训练，也可以用于真实交互。安全、成本与复位条件决定哪种交互被允许；奖励设计、观测和动作接口常比算法名称更影响结果。

A policy interacts with the environment and optimizes behavior through returns. PPO is a widely used on-policy optimization algorithm; SAC is a widely used off-policy algorithm within the maximum-entropy framework. On-policy and off-policy describe how updates use the sampling distribution, which is not the same concept as “online learning during deployment.”

PPO or SAC can be trained in simulation or used with real-world interaction. Safety, cost, and reset conditions determine which interactions are permitted; reward design and the observation and action interfaces often affect the results more than the algorithm’s name.

离线强化学习 / Offline Reinforcement Learning

离线 RL 从固定数据集学习策略，不要求在该学习阶段继续收集环境交互。CQL通过保守价值估计约束分布外动作的高估；IQL提供避免直接对分布外动作做价值最大化的一类方法。

离线数据的覆盖、奖励可信度和行为策略混合会限制改进空间。算法不能凭空恢复数据中完全没有的信息。D4RL是经典离线 RL 数据与评测入口，得分解释必须绑定具体数据集与归一化协议。

Offline RL learns a policy from a fixed dataset without requiring further environment interaction to be collected during that learning phase. CQL uses conservative value estimation to limit overestimation of out-of-distribution actions; IQL offers an approach that avoids directly maximizing values over out-of-distribution actions.

Offline-data coverage, reward reliability, and the mixture of behavior policies limit the scope for improvement. An algorithm cannot conjure up information entirely absent from the data. D4RL is a classic starting point for offline RL data and evaluation; scores must be interpreted with reference to the specific dataset and normalization protocol.

基于模型的规划与强化学习 / Model-Based Planning and Reinforcement Learning

学习环境模型后，可以直接在模型中搜索动作，也可以用想象轨迹训练策略。PETS以概率动力学模型和轨迹采样做控制；DreamerV3在潜空间世界模型中训练行为。

“训练了世界模型”“用模型规划”和“用想象数据训练策略”是三个不同步骤。主要风险包括误差累积、模型外推与策略利用模型缺陷，因而需要真实或高可信环境中的回归验证。

After learning an environment model, one can search for actions directly within the model or train a policy using imagined trajectories. PETS performs control using probabilistic dynamics models and trajectory sampling; DreamerV3 learns behavior within a latent-space world model.

“Training a world model,” “planning with the model,” and “training a policy with imagined data” are three distinct steps. The main risks include error accumulation, model extrapolation, and policies exploiting model flaws, so regression testing in real-world or highly reliable environments is necessary.

约束与安全相关学习 / Constraint-Aware and Safety-Related Learning

约束 RL 把成本或约束纳入策略优化；部署时也可用安全过滤器或模型预测控制限制动作。CBF属于控制与约束执行的重要工具，不能仅因系统用了 CBF 就把整套学习过程称为有无条件安全保证。保障依赖模型、可行性、观测与延迟等前提。

Constrained RL incorporates costs or constraints into policy optimization; safety filters or model predictive control can also restrict actions during deployment. CBFs are important tools for control and constraint enforcement. The mere use of a CBF does not justify attributing unconditional safety guarantees to the entire learning process. Guarantees depend on assumptions about the model, feasibility, observations, latency, and other factors.

适配蒸馏与持续更新 / Adaptation, Distillation, and Continual Updating

跨域与跨机体适配 / Cross-Domain and Cross-Embodiment Adaptation

在共同骨干上适配传感器、任务、身体和控制接口。Open X-Embodiment提供跨机体学习入口，RMA展示通过适应机制处理动力学变化的路线。需分别检验训练中见过与未见过的变化。

A shared backbone can be adapted to different sensors, tasks, bodies, and control interfaces. Open X-Embodiment provides a starting point for cross-embodiment learning; RMA demonstrates an approach that handles changes in dynamics through adaptation mechanisms. Variations seen during training and those not seen during training must be evaluated separately.

参数高效微调与蒸馏 / Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning and Distillation

可以更新全部参数、局部动作头或少量适配参数，也可以把昂贵教师的行为或表征转移到小模型。是否更便宜要同时计入教师调用、数据生成、训练与部署成本，不能只比较最终参数量。

One can update all parameters, an individual action head, or a small set of adaptation parameters, or transfer the behavior or representations of an expensive teacher to a smaller model. Determining whether an approach is cheaper requires accounting for teacher calls, data generation, training, and deployment costs together, rather than comparing only the final parameter counts.

持续学习与旧能力保持 / Continual Learning and Retention of Prior Capabilities

新增任务、失败示例或偏好信号进入训练后，需要检测旧任务退化、数据污染和奖励投机。重放、正则化、模块化与版本回滚是可组合手段；“自进化”应落到更新了哪些参数、技能、程序或记忆，以及如何决定保留更新。

When new tasks, failure examples, or preference signals enter training, check for degradation on old tasks, data contamination, and reward hacking. Replay, regularization, modularization, and version rollback can be combined. Claims of “self-evolution” should specify which parameters, skills, programs, or memories are updated and how the decision to retain an update is made.
